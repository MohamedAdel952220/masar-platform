Three name directions
Masar
مَسار
MasarRecommended

مسار means path / track / route in Arabic — and reads cleanly in English. It ties together every core idea at once: the child's daily path, the bus route, and the learning journey. Native-Arabic first, effortlessly bilingual, and it makes the progression mark feel inevitable rather than decorative.

daypath
دايباث
daypathYour concept

Your uploaded direction, refined. English-led and descriptive — "the path of the day." Clear to international/investor audiences. The trade-off: the Arabic "دايباث" is a transliteration with no native meaning, so it reads as a foreign brand name in the Arabic market.

Nurtura
نُرتُرا
NurturaOriginal idea

Warm and care-led, from "nurture." Soft and parent-friendly. The trade-off: it leans emotional rather than operational, says less about the day-tracking system underneath, and the meaning doesn't carry natively into Arabic.
